MyGig Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

MyGig ("MyGig," "we," "us") is a mobile app operated by Beverly Hills Playhouse, Inc. that collects performers' gig information in one place so their supporters can follow it without social media noise. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, you provide your first and last name, email address, and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not visible to us in readable form.

Profile information (optional). You may add the categories you perform in (for example, actor or musician), an organization name, and a short bio.

Gig information you post. If you create a gig, we store what you enter: title, type, description, runtime, performance dates and times, venue names, addresses, cities, states, ticketing links, and similar details.

Your activity in the app. We store which performers you have chosen to follow, which gigs you have removed from your feed, and any follow requests you have sent or received (and any you have dismissed), so your feed and requests show what you want to see.

Information stored only on your device. Display preferences (such as Compact Display), whether notifications are turned on, and whether you have seen the in-app guide are stored on your phone.

How information is shared

  • Visible to other signed-in users: your name, organization name, categories, and bio (so people can find you in Discover), and the gigs you post. Your email address is not shown to other users.
  • Not visible to other users: who you follow and which gigs you have removed from your feed. Performers are not told who follows them.
  • Follow requests: if you ask someone to follow you, they will see your name and your request in their Discover tab. You are not told whether they follow you, dismiss the request, or ignore it, and they are not told your view of it.
  • No sale, ads, or tracking. We do not sell your information, show advertising, or use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

Service providers

We use a small number of service providers to operate the app:

  • Supabase hosts our database and handles sign-in.
  • Expo delivers app updates to your device.
  • Apple distributes the app (TestFlight and the App Store).

These providers process information only as needed to provide their services to us.

Device permissions

  • Calendar: if you tap "Add to Calendar," MyGig asks for calendar access and adds that single event. MyGig does not upload or share your calendar contents.
  • Notifications: if you turn on Monday/Friday notifications, MyGig asks for permission and schedules reminders on your device. These are generated locally on your phone.

Your choices and deleting your account

You can edit your profile and your gigs at any time in the app. You can delete your account in the app under Profile → "Delete my account." Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, your gigs, and your follow list from our database. Copies may persist in routine backups for a limited time before being overwritten. You may also contact us at the address below to request deletion or ask questions about your information.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls that restrict each user to their own private data. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

MyGig is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
